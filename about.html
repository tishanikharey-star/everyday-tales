ABOUT EVERYDAY TALES

Every Life Has a Story

Everyday Tales is a place for stories, experiences, ideas and moments that make everyday life meaningful.

📖

Why Everyday Tales?

We created Everyday Tales because we believe that interesting stories aren't only found in extraordinary places.

They can be found in a morning cup of coffee, a conversation with a friend, a journey to a new place or a lesson learned from everyday life.

Our goal is to share these moments and create a place where readers can discover something meaningful in every story.

Our Mission

To tell simple, honest and meaningful stories that inspire people to slow down, look around and appreciate the world in front of them.
